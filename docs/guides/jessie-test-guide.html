Jessie test guide

Jessie books studios and rooms from Slack. This guide walks you through everything she does today, one scenario at a time, in about 30 minutes. Follow the steps, compare what you see with “What you should see”, and send a screenshot of anything that looks different.

Before you start

  1. In Slack, open your direct message with Jessie (search “Jessie” under Apps or Direct messages). Everything in this guide happens in that DM.
  2. Jessie is in testing and works one year ahead: the date picker opens in 2027, and “next Tuesday” means next Tuesday in 2027. Leave it that way. The bookings are real calendar events, and 2027 keeps them clear of real sessions.
  3. Name every project TEST plus your initials, for example TESTAB, so test bookings are easy to find. Leave the client empty, or type Test Client.
  4. While testing, messages Jessie would send to engineers and arrangers go to the developer instead. Picking a colleague as engineer will not ping them.
  5. Cancel every booking you make before you finish (scenario 14 shows how).
  6. Click any grey message in this guide, like hi, to copy it, then paste it in your DM with Jessie.

The buttons you will use

Book a roomOpens a two-step form. Step 1: date, start, end and what it’s for. Step 2: a free room and the session details.
My bookingsYour bookings for the next 7 days, including ones where you are the engineer or arranger.
ChangeOn every booking. Opens a form at the booking’s current date, time and room.
CancelOn every booking. Slack asks you to confirm first.
TypingYou can type a request or a question. Jessie answers and offers the buttons; she never books from chat on her own.
RepeatsA small box in step 1 for bookings that repeat every week, every weekday or every day.

02

Book a studio from the menu

Book

The main way to book. Jessie only offers rooms that are free and suited to the session.

  1. Tap Book a room. The form opens at step 1.
  2. Date: a weekday next week (the picker is already in 2027). Start: 2:00 PM. End: 4:00 PM.
  3. What’s it for? VO Recording. Tap Find rooms.
  4. Step 2 lists the free studios. The top one says Recommended. Keep it.
  5. Project: your TEST name. Client: leave empty. Engineer: it is set to you if you are an engineer; otherwise pick one.
  6. Optional: tick Add a note and type something. Notes go on the calendar event.
  7. Tap Book.
What you should seeThe form says “Your booking is being processed. You may close this window – we’ll notify you once it’s done.” Then ✅ Booked. with the title (e.g. TESTAB / HL), date, time and room.Your DM with Jessie gets the same card with Change and Cancel buttons.
Tip: you can close the form while it says “being processed”. The booking still goes through and the result comes to your DM.
03

Type a booking request

Book

Typing is a shortcut: Jessie reads what you wrote and opens the form with it filled in.

  1. Send book studio 7 next tuesday 2-4pm, project TESTGUIDE.
  2. Read Jessie’s reply, then tap Book a room under it.
  3. Close the form without booking (this one is just to look).
What you should see“Here’s what I have so far:” with Studio 7, the date and 2:00–4:00 PM, and the project. The form opens with the date, times and Studio 7 already in.
Tip: if the room you typed is taken, step 2 says so and offers the free ones.

My bookings

  2. Tap Next 7 days, then Previous 7 days.
  3. Also try typing what do I have next week?.

Tip: the booking from scenario 2 is next week, so you may need Next 7 days to see it.
05

Change a booking

Change
  1. On your booking from scenario 2 (in the DM or in My bookings), tap Change.
  2. The form opens at the current date, time and room. Move it one hour later: 3:00–5:00 PM.
  3. Tap Change.
  4. Open Change again and tick Change other session details. Change the project name, then tap Change.
What you should see“⏳ Changing…” with the new card, then ✅ Changed. with the new time (if you can see the studio calendar, only the new time is there). After the second change, the title shows the new project name.
Tip: if you press Change without changing anything, the form says “Nothing changed”.

07

Repeating booking

Series
  1. Tap Book a room. Pick a date next week, 10:00–11:00 AM, VO Recording.
  2. Tick Repeats. Set How often: Every week, How many times: 3. Tap Find rooms.
  3. In step 2, each room says on how many of the 3 dates it is free. Pick one that is free on all 3, fill in the project and engineer.
  4. Tap Check 3 dates, then Book 3 dates on the next screen.
What you should seeA check screen listing the 3 dates before anything is booked. After booking: “Processing your series of bookings. This may take a while…” You can close it. A DM follows: “✅ Booked 3 dates.” with the dates. Each date appears in My bookings.
Tip: a series takes about half a minute per date.
08

Celebrity Recording with a holding room and a booth

Rooms

Holding rooms and vocal booths are added on top of the studio, never instead of it.

  1. Tap Book a room. Pick a date and time, Celebrity Recording, then Find rooms.
  2. Check that the Room list only has studios (Studio F, Studio C).
  3. Tick + Holding room. Pick Likha (you can pick more than one).
  4. Tick + Vocal booth. Keep the booth it suggests.
  5. Fill in the project and engineer, then tap Book.
What you should seeThe card reads Room: Studio F + the booth, and Holding room: Likha. If you can see the studio calendar, one event holds all the rooms.Tap Change on it: the form shows Studio, Booth and Holding rooms separately, set to what you booked.
09

Localization Dubbing: studio and booth

Rooms
  1. Tap Book a room. Pick a date and time, Localization Dubbing, then Find rooms.
  2. Step 2 shows Studio and, under it, Booth. The booth starts at the studio’s usual one.
  3. Pick a different studio and watch the booth switch to that studio’s usual booth.
  4. Type a Project code (e.g. TEST-AB-01), pick an engineer, tap Book.
What you should seeThe title is the project code and the engineer’s initials (e.g. TEST-AB-01 / HL). The card lists the studio and the booth. QC sessions ask for the same details, with no booth.

14

Clean up

Cancel
  1. Tap My bookings. Use Next 7 days to reach every week you booked.
  2. Tap Cancel on each TEST booking and confirm in Slack’s pop-up.
  3. Also try typing cancel my booking next tuesday and cancel from what Jessie shows.
What you should see“⏳ Your cancellation is being processed…” under the booking, then ❌ Cancelled. with the booking’s card. When you are done, My bookings shows none of your TEST bookings.

Good to know

Recommended
The usual room for that kind of session that is free then. Other usual rooms are listed under it.
Not a usual room
A room that can be booked but isn’t one of the usual ones for the session. It books as asked.
Not free then
Usual rooms that are taken at that time. They are never offered.
⏳ … being processed
Jessie is writing to the calendar. You can close the window; the result comes to your DM.
Change other session details
Lets a change edit the project, client, engineer or arranger, not only the time and room.
Engineer and arranger notices
When you book, change or cancel a session someone else is on, they are told. During testing these go to the developer.
Who can change a booking
The person who booked it, and the engineer or arranger on it.

If something looks wrong

  1. Take a screenshot of the message or form, including the time Slack shows.
  2. Write one line on what you did and what you expected instead.
  3. Send both to the developer in Slack. Then carry on with the next scenario; one problem rarely blocks the rest.
  4. If Jessie doesn’t answer at all for a minute, note the time and tell the developer straight away.
