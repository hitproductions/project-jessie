Jessie quick start

Jessie books studios and rooms for you in Slack. Open your direct message with Jessie and say hi to get started.

01Book a room

  1. Tap Book a room.
  2. Step 1: date, start, end, and what it’s for → Find rooms.
  3. Step 2: pick a free room (the top one is recommended), add the project, client and engineer → Book.

You get ✅ Booked. in your DM with the card. You can close the form while it says “being processed”.

02See, change or cancel

  • My bookings shows your next 7 days, including sessions you engineer or arrange.
  • Change moves the date, time or room. Tick Change other session details for the project, client, engineer or arranger.
  • Cancel asks you to confirm first.

03Type instead

Jessie reads what you type and opens the form filled in, or shows the booking with its buttons.

  • book studio 7 next tuesday 2-4pm, project ORANGE
  • make my ORANGE booking 5-6pm
  • cancel my booking next tuesday

Nothing is booked or changed until you press the button in the form.

04Ask questions

  • is studio 8 free tomorrow 3-5pm?
  • what's free friday afternoon?
  • what do I have this week?
  • which rooms can do 5.1?

05Repeating bookings

In step 1, tick Repeats: every week (pick the days), every weekday or every day, for a number of times or until a date. Every date is checked first, then Book N dates.

06Extra rooms and special cases

  • + Vocal booth on recording sessions, + Holding room on Celebrity Recording - booked together with the studio.
  • Localization Dubbing: pick the Studio and the Booth; the booth starts at the studio’s usual one.
  • M booth: tick All day booking for the whole day.
  • Another room… at the bottom of the list for a room that isn’t a usual one.
  • Music sessions: an engineer, an arranger, or both.
