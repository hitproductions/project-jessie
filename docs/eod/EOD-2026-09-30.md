## What was found, and how

- **The forgotten date was a race, not the date logic (gotcha 26).** Gate Context keeps the date under discussion in
  workflow static data, which n8n saves only when an execution finishes - and since the Turn Log, a turn runs on after
  its reply. The Turn Log showed it: v194's "no client" came back MISSING_DATE 3 s after the previous row; v195's, with
  the same gap, carried the date. v195 re-reads the date from the requester's own earlier messages when nothing is
  carried.
- **A trailing line break in Bookers Info hid a nickname (gotcha 27).** Four Infos end in one (Angelo Villegas, Rico
  Gonzales, Alec Elijah Gan, Cristel Cube); the "Goes by" reader matched none of them and lost the first nickname
  ("for enrico" found no one). Fixed in main v196 and Book Session v70.
- **Clients were only searched by Name.** A client's "Goes by" nickname lives in Notes. v196's Clients tool searches it;
  Book Session v70 reads the clients with a "Goes by" (one today) to spot a nickname shared with staff.

## Decisions today

- **Session-type shorthand** (ISR = In-Studio Recording = VO Recording) is asked about once, never used as a project or
  client unless the requester says "project ISR" / "client ISR". More nicknames can come from an "Also known as" field
  on Session Types (optional; needs a small main change).
- **A shared nickname is asked about:** "by Anj, do you mean Angelo Villegas (Music Arranger) or Angela Dela Calzada
  (Advertising Producer, a client)?" For this to work for other people, a client who shares a nickname with staff needs
  a "Goes by ..." line in Clients Notes (Tel).
- **A booked-for colleague is not the engineer or the client** unless named as such (or, for the engineer, an engineer
  by role; for the client, a client on record).
- **No engineer named and the requester is an engineer: the requester engineers**, with a note on the summary saying so.
