## The booking-type bug, in plain words

When Book is pressed, the form hands Book Session a short sentence describing the booking - its type, room, project, client,
engineer, arranger and department. Book Session still ran a safety check from the chat days: it read that sentence for booking-type
names and, if it seemed to name two, asked "Which one?" (shown in the form as "Couldn't tell which booking type this is"). It leaves out
general words like "recording" and "session", so "Music Recording" is just "music" to it. So:
- Van Recording with an arranger: the department became "Music" - the check saw Van Recording *and* Music Recording, and stopped.
- A project or client name with another type's word did the same: VO Recording for "band demo" looked like Band Recording, "event
  teaser" like Event; Music Recording for "mix tape" was even switched to Music Mixing. 361 of 598 combinations were refused.

The fix (Book Session v121): in the form the type is picked from a list, so it is known for sure - if it is in the sentence (from the
form it always is), it wins, whatever other words are there. The check still asks only when a type really wasn't given.
