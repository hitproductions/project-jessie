## Decided today

- **No confirmation is typed.** Every yes / no is a button or a form; each chat card goes when its replacement lands. A
  card already sent before today's builds can still be answered with yes.
- **Anything that is not a booking, a question or an answer gets the menu** - unless Jessie is waiting for an answer.
- Series built right after step C; the chat's series card stays only for cards already sent.
- The Booked message is the card: title, then Date, Time, Room. Notes go on the calendar event, behind a checkbox in
  the form. Only the top room in step 2 carries a note (a series adds "free on k of N dates").
