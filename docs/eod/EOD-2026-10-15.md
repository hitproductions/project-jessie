## Decided today

- **No confirmation is typed.** Every yes / no is a button or a form; each chat card goes when its replacement lands. A
  card already sent before today's builds can still be answered with yes.
- **Every message leads to the Book a room / My bookings buttons.** The AI answers questions only (rooms, what is free, what
  is booked, people, session types, clients) and never asks for booking details; anything that is not a booking, a
  question or an answer gets the menu.
- Series built right after step C; the chat's series card stays only for cards already sent.
- The engineer and arranger are told when someone else books them, adds them, or changes / cancels their session - the
  booker's own changes included; nobody is told about their own change. During QA, to Howard.
- A change can be the details alone (project, client, engineer, arranger), with the same checks as a new booking.
- "Producer" in a booking means its client. Every Book a room has My bookings beside it.
- The Booked message is the card: title, then Date, Time, Room. Notes go on the calendar event, behind a checkbox in
  the form. Only the top room in step 2 carries a note (a series adds "free on k of N dates").
- **Holding rooms and vocal booths are booked on top of the studio**, never instead of it; Localization Dubbing picks its
  studio and booth separately, the booth starting at the studio's usual pair.
- **QC** takes the Localization details. **Post Processing**: the booker is set to Dylan Diaz for now, a project title,
  no client; anyone can pick it for now.
- **M booth all day** is either all day or the chosen times. An all-day booking is a 12:00 AM to 12:00 AM event (Google
  shows it in the all-day banner) - fine as it is.
- **Music Mixing / Music Vocal Recording** may have no engineer: an engineer, an arranger, or both, never neither.
- "Use another room" is the last choice in the room list. Working lines don't repeat "this takes a few seconds".
- A session-length note under a card is small text, and in hours (round 6).
