## Processing times - measured by Howard in Slack, 29 Oct

From tapping the button (or sending the message) to the result. Bookings: from submitting the form to the booking
confirmation, by which point the event is on the calendar.

| Request | Seconds |
|---|---|
| "hi" | 5 |
| A typed request with details (comes back as the form, filled in) | 7 |
| My bookings | 6 |
| Find rooms (step 1 -> step 2), one date | 5 |
| Find rooms, repeating, 4 dates | 9 |
| Find rooms, repeating, 10 dates | 18 |
| Book one room: studio, VO Recording (project, client, engineer) | 11 |
| Book one room: meeting room | 9 |
| Book one room: M booth | 9 |
| Book studio + vocal booth, Localization Dubbing (project code, engineer) | 9 |
| Book studio + vocal booth + 2 holding rooms, Celebrity Recording (project, engineer, arranger, client) | 13 |
| Repeating, 4 dates, 1 studio, Localization Mixing | 19 |
| Repeating, 4 dates, studio + vocal booth, Localization Dubbing | 16 (written "1 - 16"; 16 s on the slide - to confirm) |
| Repeating, 10 weekdays, Localization Editing | 30 |
| Cancel | 10 |

Change was not timed. Several rooms on one date take about the same as one room (one check, one event); a series grows with
its dates.

**Where these numbers live, for next time:**
- The **Turn Log** tab of the Jessie Booking Log sheet is written only by main (typed messages): its Seconds column runs from
  the Slack message's own timestamp to just after Jessie's reply. A typed request's row is the form offer, not a booking.
- Form submissions run in Slack Interactions, which writes no Turn Log row. Its **Timing Sample** node (each run, in
  Executions) gives `per` = seconds from n8n receiving the tap to the result on screen (book: tap Book -> "Booked."; change:
  Save -> "Changed."; rooms: Find rooms -> step 2; series per date); To Prepare's `rooms` says one room or several.
- The **Jessie Timings** n8n data table keeps one running "9 in 10 finish within" figure per scenario (rooms, rooms-series,
  book, check, series, change) - the "Usually under …" line. Single and multi-room bookings share the `book` row; Cancel is
  not timed.
- The rooms' own acceptance of the invitation comes a few seconds after "Booked." (Room Check reads at ~2 / 6 / 16 s) and is
  not timed anywhere.

## Approved for launch

Howard presented the deck to management ("JESSIE Launch Update 1029", the same Slides artifact, retitled) and Jessie was
**approved for launch**. The date is to be set. What the presented version said:

- **Jessie:** Hit Productions' bookings manager - "Manage KDC bookings in Slack": message, fill out forms, get notifications;
  lives in Google Calendar (KDC Bookings stays); understands each booking type and prevents double bookings.
- **Journey, v1 to v287:** from a chatbot to chat + forms. Kept: Google Calendar and Airtable, the booking workflows, typed
  details, inquiries. New: forms and buttons (controlled input). Changed: Gemini -> Claude Haiku, less reliance on the AI,
  cheaper, faster, more predictable. Dropped: free-form chatting.
- **Functions:** Book, Change, Cancel, My bookings, Inquiries.
- **API cost:** Claude Haiku 5.5, in testing and development since 8 Oct; the AI interprets typed messages and database
  lookups and answers questions.
- **Processing time** (the table above; VO Recording shown as 12 s): shortest 5 s (first message), longest 30 s (a 10-date
  series), a common request 12 s.
- **Challenges:** usage at full-scale volume (simultaneous bookings, many users), outages / connectivity, database
  maintenance, the fallback scenario. To consider: a beta period with limited users - a dual run of Jessie and Google
  Calendar for about two weeks, with the backfill issue (hand-made bookings).
- **Ready for launch?** Backfill non-Jessie bookings; the switches (the default year 2027 -> 2026, notification and reminder
  recipients); Google Calendar permissions to view-only; roll-out.
- **User adoption:** the End-user Manual, the Quick Start guide, a video tutorial.
- Then the screenshot walkthrough: booking (date and time, recommendation, details, confirm), changing (details, time,
