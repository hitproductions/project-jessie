  created; a room that declines is reported (DM) and its event deleted. (To double-check that tablet bookings reach KDC
  Bookings.)

  both boxes; an arranger is an arranger only (Bookers checked: no one is listed as both - the form already does this).

  booths and meeting rooms; they also go to a staff member named in *Who is it for?*.

  they are built. (Reminders v2, live 21 Oct ~00:07.)
