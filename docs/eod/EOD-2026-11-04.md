## Series by design (PENDING 150) - proposal

Today a series is checked one date at a time (Room Availability per date, ~3 s each) and booked one date at a time (Book Series calls
Book Session per date, which re-reads the reference data, re-checks the people and the client, reads the calendar and creates the
event - a few seconds each). That is why the cap is 16. At six months (Weekly ~26 dates, Weekdays ~130, Daily ~180) it would take minutes.

What does not need to happen per date:
1. **One read, every date.** One read of KDC Bookings for the whole span (first date to last) and one room free/busy call (all rooms,
   the same span) - then every date's free rooms are worked out in code. Seconds for any length, not 3 s a date.
2. **Check the details once.** The title, client, people, booking type and department are the same on every date - checked once
   up front; only the room and the time are per date.
3. **Create the events together.** Re-read the span once right before booking (someone may have booked in between), then create the
   events in parallel batches instead of one after another.

That also makes the review screen cheap (Howard's "C"): the dates are already worked out when step 2 opens, so it can show
"24 of 26 dates free" and list only the problem dates, each with its own choice - another free studio that day, another time that day,
or skip that date - then Book. 20 dates or more: "You're about to book N appointments" before it books. The six-month cap replaces 16.
Slack's 100-block limit means a long series lists its problem dates, not every date.

Order proposed: 149's import and Slack test -> this (one build across Slack Interactions, Book Series, Room Availability) -> 152 (skip
step 1) -> 118 (series cancel / edit, which needs the series tag this build would add).
