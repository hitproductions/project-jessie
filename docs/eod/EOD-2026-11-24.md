## Hand-made bookings by initials (PENDING 161) - built, not imported

A coordinator made a session by hand in Google Calendar for an engineer ("ATE / Client / HL"). It did not show in the engineer's
My bookings: since PENDING 155 (19 Nov) a booking made by hand belongs only to whoever created it.

Decided today:
- **The title's initials count, only on events Jessie did not make** (no `ref:` and no `team:` on the first line). Jessie's own
  bookings are read from their own fields exactly as before, so booking through Jessie stays the standard - the initials never add
  anyone to a Jessie booking.
- Only a last title part after " / " or " | " made entirely of initials counts ("HL", "HL x MV", "HL AEG"); "M1 - Rico",
  "SALIN - MUSIC DEPT", "NET | PANDA ANI" never.
- **Option (b):** the initials' owner sees it in My bookings ("You're on the team") and may change or cancel it; the creator is told
  when they do. Move's replacement carries "Booked by: <creator> | ref | team: <initials' owners>", so neither loses it after a change.
- **Reminders:** the initials' owner gets the 4 PM / Monday reminder when the booking names nobody (no `team:`, Engineer: or Arranger:).
- **Shared initials** (two people in Bookers have LS): they go to the one with Client Booking authority - the one who books studios.
  No Airtable change.
