  then failed at the yes on a shorter copy of the requester's messages. A yes to a verified card is not re-checked.

  question).

  "thank you" became "thank", and the model's "THANK" survived because the requester did type that word.
- **An import pasted over the canvas** is caught by `check-import after` (yesterday's v82) - no repeat today.
