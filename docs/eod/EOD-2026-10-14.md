## Afternoon: the design, settled

`docs/design/slack-app-v1-scope.md` (build scope: **slice 1** = the everyday loop, built and tested first; **slice 2**
completes the release; then optional), and a click-through prototype of slice 1,
`docs/design/slack-app-slice1-prototype.html` (14 flows, real Block Kit, taps per flow). Decided along the way:
- No "Booking for" field - the engineer / arranger fields say who it is for; **the booker and the assigned engineer and
  arranger can change or cancel a booking** (their Slack ids saved on the event; two Bookers share the initials LS).
- **Change** replaces "Move" (date, time, length - a session running over - or room). The Change form starts at the
  booking's current values; pressing Change with nothing changed says "Nothing changed" and never re-books.
- **Series is in slice 1** (Localization books dubbing in batches; also mixing, post, M booths, meetings). A small
  *Repeats* box on every step 1.
- **Studio and booth picked separately** (any combination, the studio's usual booth first). Recording sessions get small
  *+ Vocal booth* / *+ Holding room* lines.
- My bookings pages week by week (Next / Previous 7 days). Nobody is told they were told. No tentative / hold bookings.
- The "room turns the booking down" case is a back-end safeguard only, not a scenario.
