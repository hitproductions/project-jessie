# Jessie QA – 22 Oct (brief for Camy)

**What's new since your last round:** Jessie is now a Slack app. Everything that books or changes something is a **form or a
button**, so there are no more typed "yes / no" replies. You can still type a request ("book studio 7 tomorrow 2-4pm") or a
question ("what's free tomorrow?"), and Jessie answers or opens the form for you.

**How to start:** open your DM with Jessie and type `hi`. You get **Book a room** and **My bookings**.

**Good to know**
- Dates land in **2027** on purpose (QA year shift). A date you pick in the form is already 2027.
- You get your own messages: your Booked cards, your reminders, and any **room request** for a booking you made.
- At **4:00 PM** you'll get a reminder for tomorrow's studio bookings. Book one in the morning so it has something to remind you
  about (sheet R1).
- The **room request** tests (Q) need Howard: he asks for a room you booked, and you answer with the buttons. Tell him when your
  booking is ready.

**How to fill in the sheet** (tab *camy v5*): one row per test. Write what actually happened in your own words, mark *Worked?*
YES or NO, and add a screenshot link in *Notes* if something looks off. If something deletes or changes a booking you didn't ask
it to, that's a **STOP**: stop that section and message Howard.

**At the end:** My bookings → cancel every test booking you made.

Thanks!
