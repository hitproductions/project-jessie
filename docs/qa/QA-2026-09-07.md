## A1 — FAIL: the room list is non-deterministic

The sheet's stated check **passed**: no room with `Active / Bookable` unticked ever
appeared. M2 and M6 stayed out of all eleven answers. The known switched-off-rooms bug
did not fire once.

The failure is the opposite direction — **bookable, free rooms get silently dropped**,
and not consistently. Eleven asks of the same question produced three different
answers:

| Variant | Rooms | Times seen | Correct? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Likha, Lobby, M1, M3, M5, M7 + all 15 Studios | **21** | 7 | ✅ correct and complete |
| all 15 Studios only | 15 | 3 | ❌ drops Likha, Lobby, M1, M3, M5, M7 |
| Likha + all 15 Studios | 16 | 1 | ❌ drops Lobby, M1, M3, M5, M7 |

So the correct answer **is** reachable — it came out 7 times in 10 under load — but
roughly a third of the time the non-`Studio`-prefixed rooms are dropped. The likely
mechanism is the model reading "what **studios** are free" literally and filtering to
rooms whose name begins with "Studio", inconsistently.

**The computation is not at fault.** `Compute Availability` ran on all ten burst turns
in under 101 ms with no failures, and the correct 21-room result was clearly available
to the model. This is the presentation layer dropping rooms after the fact.

That places it squarely under the project's central principle in `CLAUDE.md`:
*everything the prompt merely asks for is unreliable.* Listing the free rooms is
currently left to the model, and it fails about a third of the time.

Note the earlier serial run (17:16) produced the 16-room variant, which is why this
first looked like a systematic exclusion rather than a coin flip. It took ten repeats
to see what it actually is.

### Also cosmetic, same cause

The ten answers used six different phrasings — "Free in that window:", "Free next
Thursday (September 16) from...", "Next Thursday, September 16, from 2:00 PM to
5:00 PM, these rooms are free:", and others. Harmless, but it confirms nothing binds
the shape of this reply.

## Confirmed working, beyond the sheet

**The `Guard Probe` weekday fix is now proven live.** `September 16, 2027 (Thu)`
printed correctly in both the booking summary and the cancel summary. That fix shipped
on 3 September and was verified offline across 8 cases, but no live turn had reached a
date since — the `Thunesday` bug is dead in production.

**Every guard ran under load.** `Guard Probe` executed on 10 of 10 burst turns with
zero failures. Since a failing Code node fails open — replies still go out, just
unprocessed — this was the risk worth checking, and it held.

**`Get Booker` latency has a cause.** Its recorded 8.5 s spikes have never been
explained. Under the burst it tripled from ~1.2 s to 3.36 s, so concurrency is the
likely mechanism.
