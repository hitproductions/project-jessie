What it does today

Everything starts from two buttons

Book a room

Two steps: when and what for, then a free room and the details. The best room is recommended.

My bookings

Your next 7 days, including sessions where you are the engineer or arranger.

Change and cancel

Buttons on every booking. Change the time, room or details; cancel asks first.

Repeating bookings

Every week, weekday or day. Each date is checked before anything is booked.

Paired rooms

Vocal booths and holding rooms booked with the studio. Localization pairs studio and booth.

Type or ask

A typed request opens the form filled in. Questions get short, plain answers.

What Jessie does today. Everything starts from two buttons in your DM: Book a room and My bookings. Booking is two steps, and only free, suitable rooms are offered, with the best one recommended. My bookings shows your next seven days, including sessions you engineer or arrange. Every booking has Change and Cancel. Repeating bookings check every date first. Vocal booths and holding rooms are booked together with the studio. And you can still type: a request opens the form already filled in, and questions get plain answers.
