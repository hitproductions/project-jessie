Why we changed course

A chatbot is only as reliable as the conversation

When the first message has it all

Room, date, time and project in one go: the booking comes out right.

When details arrive bit by bit

Long back-and-forth, typos and vague requests: the AI starts to misread what people mean.

Everyone asks differently, so free-form chat makes every exchange unpredictable.

The chatbot worked well when someone gave it everything up front: the room, the date, the time, the project. The booking came out right. But real conversations are rarely that tidy. When details came in bit by bit, over a long back-and-forth, with typos or vague requests, the AI started to misunderstand. Because everyone phrases things differently, every exchange was unpredictable.
