Before and after the pivot

What we keep, gain, lose and change

Keep

  • Typing a request to start
  • Asking questions in chat
  • Every booking rule and check we built
  • The studio calendar and Airtable as the source

Gain

  • Predictable, repeatable results
  • Fewer misunderstandings
  • Faster fixes, less upkeep
  • Our booking workflow enforced by the form

Lose

  • Chat-exclusive experience of booking

Change

  • Confirm with a button, not by typing “yes”
  • Dates, times and rooms picked from lists
  • Change and Cancel buttons on every booking
  • The AI answers questions and interprets initial input only
To sum up the pivot. We keep: typing a request to start, asking questions, every booking rule and check we built, and the calendar and Airtable as the source of truth. We gain: predictable results, fewer misunderstandings, faster fixes and less upkeep, and a form that enforces our workflow. We lose: some of the open-ended conversation, a few more taps for a quick booking, and testing now needs people pressing buttons. What changes: you confirm with a button instead of typing yes, you pick dates, times and rooms from lists, every booking has Change and Cancel, and the AI only answers questions.
